About the lab

Sport psychology.
From the field.

The Sport Psychology Lab studies how an athlete's mind shapes performance. We measure psychological factors such as metacognition, self-talk, grit and flow, and interpret the experiences of athletes and coaches through qualitative research. We then return these findings to the field through sport psychology counseling and coaching.

The lab grew out of years of field experience coaching Korea's national taekwondo poomsae team. Our work now extends from the embodied knowledge of sport to the psychology of coaches and organizations.

Every topic leads back to one question:
how do athletes understand and regulate their own performance?
